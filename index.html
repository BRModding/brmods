How to install modsStep-by-step: PC version + Link / Air Link →

Gorilla Tag mods run on the PC version of the game. You play it on your Quest by streaming from your PC with Link or Air Link. Guides that promise mods on the standalone Quest app are usually outdated or unsafe.

  1. Get Gorilla Tag on PC: install the free PC version from Steam or the Meta Quest PC app.
  2. Connect your Quest: use a Link cable or Air Link and check that the game opens in your headset.
  3. Install a mod manager such as Monke Mod Manager, from its official GitHub page. It installs the mod loader (BepInEx) for you.
  4. Launch the game once and close it. This creates the BepInEx/plugins folder.
  5. Add mods with the manager or drop their .dll files into BepInEx/plugins. Only use them in modded or private lobbies.
Will I get banned?Read this before you mod →

Cheats, mod menus and anything that gives you an advantage get you banned, including in modded lobbies. Modding isn't officially supported, so there's always some risk. Cosmetic and fun mods in private or modded lobbies are much safer. This site never lists cheats.

Mods broke after an update?Fixes & how to remove all mods →

Updates often break mods until their authors catch up. Update your mods, and if the game won't start, remove the newest mod from BepInEx/plugins. To remove everything, delete the BepInEx folder and winhttp.dll from the game folder, or use "Verify integrity of game files" in Steam.

Is it safe?Avoid fake "Quest mod" downloads →

This site only links to each mod's own page and doesn't host files. Prefer open-source mods on GitHub. "No PC" mod APKs from TikTok or file-sharing sites often carry malware, and never run anything that asks you to turn off your antivirus.
